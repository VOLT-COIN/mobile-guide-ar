Privacy Policy

The site does not ask for personal data and has no sign-up. When you open a page, your browser connects directly to Binance to get prices, and asks the site server (Cloudflare) for news, which fetches it from the news sites. These services may record your IP address, like any website.

Your favorites list, your portfolio (the amounts and buy prices you type on the Tools page) and your light/dark choice are saved only in your browser (local storage) and are not sent to us.

Ads from third parties such as Google AdSense may be shown in the future and may use cookies. Right now no ads are enabled.

Visitor counter: when you open the site, a request is sent to the site server (Cloudflare) to raise an aggregate counter for your country (Cloudflare determines the country from your connection). We do not store your IP address or any identifier about you in the database, only numbers per country. To know whether a browser is new, we store a small flag in your browser local storage.
