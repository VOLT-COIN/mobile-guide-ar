About Chainbeat

Chainbeat shows prices of popular cryptocurrencies and the latest headlines about them. Prices come straight to your browser from the public market data API of Binance. News comes from the public RSS feeds of four known sites (Cointelegraph, CoinDesk, Decrypt and Bitcoin Magazine) and appears as headlines and links to the original source.

Updates: prices update as soon as they arrive from Binance over a live connection, and if that fails they refresh every 10 seconds. The site server fetches the public RSS feeds about every 5 minutes, and only crypto-related headlines are shown, with the date the source published them. If a source goes down, a notice appears at the top of the page.

It does not give investment advice.
